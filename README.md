# 📊 YouTube Data Insights Report 2025 – Power BI Dashboard

A complete end-to-end **Power BI project** analyzing YouTube media performance, revenue trends, and audience engagement — designed with an approach similar to **FMCG business intelligence frameworks**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This report transforms raw YouTube analytics data into **actionable insights** using Power BI.  
It demonstrates how data visualization, storytelling, and BI modeling can help content creators, marketers, and FMCG-style businesses make data-driven decisions.

**Goal:**  
To design a professional 8-page Power BI dashboard covering KPIs, engagement analysis, revenue tracking, and predictive insights.

---

## 🧱 Tech Stack
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop 
- **Modeling:** Star Schema (Fact + Dimension Tables)  
- **Visualization:** DAX Measures, Custom Charts, Map Visuals  
- **Forecasting:** Power BI’s Time-Series Models  

---

## 📂 Dashboard Structure (8 Pages)

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview
- Total Views: 505M+  
- Total Revenue: $2.78M  
- Avg CTR: 5.75% | Engagement Rate: 7%  
- Subscribers: 250K+  
🔹 *FMCG Analogy:* High-level KPI view similar to a sales performance dashboard.

---

### 2️⃣ Channel Performance Deep Dive
- Avg CPM ≈ $5.5–5.6  
- Engagement Rate: 7.3–7.5%  
- Likes: 27.9M | Comments: 4.0M | Shares: 5.3M  
🔹 *Insight:* Channel-level ROI and engagement — like product-line profitability in FMCG.

---

### 3️⃣ Category & Content Insights
- Top Categories: Travel, Music, Vlogging  
- Optimal Duration: 340K–360K mins → higher revenue  
🔹 *Insight:* Identify best-performing content length & niche (duration optimization).

---

### 4️⃣ Audience Demographics & Consumer Behavior
- Gender Split: 50% Male / 50% Female  
- Top Cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad (~33% each)  
🔹 *Insight:* Geo-demographic segmentation for targeting and strategy.

---

### 5️⃣ Engagement & Sentiment Analysis
- Likes > 3.5M | Dislikes ~0.4M  
- Engagement ↔ Revenue correlation: 7.3–7.5%  
🔹 *Insight:* Positive sentiment drives monetization — mirrors brand loyalty tracking.

---

### 6️⃣ Revenue & ROI Analysis
- Total Revenue: $2.78M | Avg CPM: $5.50  
- Equal city revenue distribution (~$0.93M each)  
🔹 *Insight:* Regional ROI tracking similar to market performance dashboards in FMCG.

---

### 7️⃣ City Performance Insights
- Each city contributes ≈33% to overall metrics  
- Watch Time: ~10M hrs per city  
🔹 *Insight:* Balanced urban viewership — opportunity for localized content planning.

---

### 8️⃣ Age Group Performance
- Core audience: 18–34 yrs (dominant segment)  
🔹 *Insight:* Age-based targeting for content and ad strategy.

---

## 📈 Key Business Learnings
✅ Data storytelling can connect analytics with strategy.  
✅ Regional segmentation helps design targeted campaigns.  
✅ Engagement rate directly influences ad revenue.  
✅ Predictive insights (AI layer) guide future upload & investment planning.
